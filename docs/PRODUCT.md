# Product Context

Personal Finance Tracker is primarily a single-user personal net-worth and portfolio tracker. Its primary purpose is useful, trustworthy personal finance tracking. Its secondary purpose is practical learning through AI-assisted software development. Broader product or business use is currently tertiary.

## Principles

- Trustworthy before sophisticated.
- Keep investment and wealth growth first: focus on understanding, managing, and growing wealth and investments rather than becoming a generic household-finance app.
- Clarity at a glance: make the user's wealth, portfolio structure, and important changes immediately understandable rather than merely presenting financial data.
- Prefer low maintenance, while allowing additional manual input when it materially improves accuracy, insight, or usefulness.
- Preserve practical manual control and fallbacks.
- Preserve financial data safely.
- Useful over comprehensive.

## Current supported scope

The product supports accounts and cash, holdings/assets, first-class pensions, fixed-rate deposits with calculated accrual, ILS/USD and FX, liquidity, portfolio classifications, manual and supported automatic market pricing, snapshots/history, external wealth-flow tracking, archive/restore/delete safeguards, backup/restore, an internal change log, Hebrew/English, and responsive UI.

It represents current balances and holdings rather than a complete transaction ledger. External wealth flows record money crossing the boundary of all tracked wealth, but the product is not a full record of every transfer, trade, fee, dividend, or realized transaction.

## Current non-goals

- Full expense tracking and budgeting
- Automatic bank or broker synchronization
- Full transaction accounting
- Tax reporting
- Detailed dividend or realized-return accounting
- Financial forecasting
- AI financial advice
